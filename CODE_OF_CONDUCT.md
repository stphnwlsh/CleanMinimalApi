# Code of Conduct

We want Clean Minimal API to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and keep the focus on what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository's [security advisories](https://github.com/stphnwlsh/CleanMinimalApi/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer, [@stphnwlsh](https://github.com/stphnwlsh). The maintainer will review reports and may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block accounts that break these expectations.
